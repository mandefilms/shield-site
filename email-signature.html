To add it to Gmail (on a computer):

  1. Click "Copy signature" above (or select the signature with your mouse and copy it).
  2. In Gmail, click the gear icon, then "See all settings".
  3. Scroll to "Signature", click "Create new", name it "Shield", and paste into the box.
  4. Under "Signature defaults", choose it for new emails and replies.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click "Save Changes".
